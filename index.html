About

A physician for the whole course of treatment

Dr Thanish is a medical oncologist, a doctor who treats cancer with medicines. That includes chemotherapy, immunotherapy, targeted therapy and hormone therapy, along with the supportive care that keeps treatment safe and on track.

Dr Thanish holds an MBBS, an MD and a DM in Medical Oncology, the super-speciality degree dedicated to the medical treatment of cancer. Every plan starts from the pathology report and stage, uses the tumour's molecular profile where it matters, and follows current international guidelines such as NCCN and ESMO.

Patients and families get clear explanations, time for their questions, and a plan they understand before treatment begins.

Approach

How a treatment plan is made

Each step answers a question the next one depends on.

  1. Confirm the diagnosis

    Biopsy review with histopathology and immunohistochemistry, so treatment starts from the right diagnosis.

  2. Stage the cancer

    CT, MRI or PET-CT to map the extent of disease, recorded as a TNM stage.

  3. Profile the tumour

    Biomarker and molecular tests such as EGFR, ALK, HER2, PD-L1 or BRCA, when the result changes treatment.

  4. Plan with the team

    Your case is discussed at a multidisciplinary tumour board, then the options are explained so you can decide together.

  5. Treat and review

    Treatment follows NCCN and ESMO guidelines. Response is checked on scans at set intervals and the plan is adjusted if needed.

  6. Follow-up and survivorship

    Regular follow-up after treatment, with attention to late effects, recovery and quality of life.

Feeling unwell during chemotherapy?

A temperature of 38 °C or higher, bleeding, breathlessness or chest pain needs urgent attention. Go to the nearest emergency department or call your treating team straight away. Do not wait for a reply to this form.
